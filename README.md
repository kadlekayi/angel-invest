# Development & Social Impact Tracker
Angel investors and startups addressing institutional voids (Social Entrepreneurship class definition)

## Files
- `investors.csv` — 16 angel networks, individual angels, and adjacent early-stage impact investors
- `startups.csv` — 16 startups/ventures filling institutional voids with meaningful social impact

## Inclusion criteria (per class definition)
A company or investor qualifies if it:
1. Focuses on an **institutional void** — a gap where formal institutions (banks, courts, utilities, healthcare systems, supply chains, education systems) fail to serve a population, and
2. Creates **meaningful, measurable social impact** through its core business model (not CSR on the side).

## Sector landscape overview
The development-focused social entrepreneurship ecosystem clusters around a handful of recurring institutional voids:

1. **Financial inclusion** (fintech for the unbanked) — the deepest vein. Examples: MNT-Halan (Egypt), Wave (Senegal), Oraan (Pakistan), Lula (South Africa). Backed by Accion Venture Lab, LeapFrog, and local angel networks like I3N.
2. **Energy access** (off-grid solar) — Acumen's Hardest-to-Reach initiative alone committed ~$246M in 2025; Yellow, Bboxx.
3. **Agricultural supply chains & finance** — Root Capital on the debt side; Winich, Complete Farmer, Acceso, SmartMoney on the platform side.
4. **Healthcare access** — Ilara Health (Kenya) and similar clinic-enablement models.
5. **Education/childcare** — Kidogo (East Africa), backed by Acumen and Pivotal Ventures.

On the capital side, the angel landscape is thinner than mainstream VC but has distinct nodes:
- **Global networks**: Toniic (~500 members, 25+ countries), Social Venture Circle (US), Acumen's angel ecosystem
- **Regional**: E8 Angels (cleantech), Angels of Impact (women-led enterprises, APAC), I3N/Intellecap (India), Katapult (Nordic/global)
- **Structural gaps**: the "pioneer gap" (too risky for VCs, too big for philanthropy) and the "missing middle" (too big for microfinance, too small for PE/funds) — the best angels in this space explicitly position themselves in those gaps.
